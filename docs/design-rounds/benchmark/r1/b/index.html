BBenchmark page · rung 1 LAYOUT · axis "dossier"

Thesis: the page is a compact ranked list with a persistent dossier beside it. One row is always selected, and its measurement conditions are always on screen. Scan on the left, read and cite on the right. Labelled boxes only: no data, no style.
