Axis: what leads the page. Here the measurement conditions and the caveat lead; the table follows as the evidence for them. The two measurement regimes ("Measured by us", "Published by Open Code Review") are cards at the top, and the table's "Measured by" filter is driven from those cards.

Rank choice: rank numbers, no medals. The rank is the position in the current sort and filter, recomputed on every change; the regime tag sits in the cell next to it, so no rank reads without its regime.

Reading: grey boxes are zones; bold is the zone name, the grey line is what it holds. Dashed lines mark the first screen (1440 by 900 desktop, 390 by 844 phone). Boards drawn at 1:1 scale. No data, no styling.
