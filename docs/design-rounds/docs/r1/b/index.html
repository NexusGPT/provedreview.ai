What B is worse at

  • The whole tree is never visible on a page: the left list shows one section. A reader who does not know which section holds a page guesses a tab or searches. Only the /docs/ landing lists every page.
  • Two bars stacked above the reading column cost vertical space on every page; on the phone, the section strip plus the pages menu bar take about two rows more than a single menu would.
  • Six tab labels of uneven length ("Differential attestation" against "FAQ") and uneven section sizes (nine DA pages against one FAQ page). At 390 the strip hides tabs 4 to 6 off-screen until the reader scrolls it.
  • The in-page table of contents lives inside a 240 px list, nested under the open page: long pages with many headings make that list tall and wrap the heading titles.
