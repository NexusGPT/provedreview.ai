ADocs, rung 1 (layout): rail

Axis: a fixed left rail of every section and page, a wide reading column, an in-page table of contents on the right for long pages. The rail is the map: where am I, what else is here, all at once, with no click. Boxes are zones only: no data, no styling. Frames are drawn at true width (1440 and 390 CSS px); the dashed line marks the first screen.
